# AI-GAMNET — Gambia & Africa AI Community

AI-GAMNET is a growing community network that connects researchers, students, practitioners, and innovators working to advance ethical, inclusive, and impactful artificial intelligence in The Gambia and across Africa.

The organisation brings together people working across health, agriculture, education, governance, and other key sectors to build local capacity, share knowledge, and create opportunities for collaboration.

## Our mission

AI-GAMNET exists to strengthen the AI ecosystem in The Gambia and the wider African context by:

- building a network of researchers, professionals, and students
- promoting learning, mentorship, and practical skills development
- supporting events, workshops, and community conversations around responsible AI
- connecting local talent with regional and international opportunities
- advancing AI solutions that respond to real societal challenges

## What we do

AI-GAMNET runs a range of programs and community activities designed to grow the field of AI in practical and impactful ways.

### Community and membership

Members join a vibrant network that includes researchers, innovators, students, and professionals interested in AI for societal impact. Membership creates access to community events, opportunities for collaboration, and ongoing engagement with the wider AI ecosystem.

### Conferences and flagship events

The network hosts major events such as Deep Learning IndabaX Gambia and other community-led gatherings that bring together experts, students, and partners to exchange ideas, showcase work, and build momentum around AI innovation.

### Meetups and webinars

AI-GAMNET regularly hosts meetups and webinars focused on emerging AI topics, applied machine learning, research, and career development. These sessions provide a platform for learning, exchange, and networking.

### Capacity building

The organisation supports the development of skills and confidence across the AI community through knowledge-sharing, mentorship, and collaborative learning opportunities that help members contribute meaningfully to the field.

## Why it matters

AI is increasingly shaping the future of work, public systems, innovation, and development. AI-GAMNET helps ensure that this transformation is informed by local realities, ethical practice, and inclusive participation.

By creating a strong pan-African community, the network helps move AI from ideas and research into practical action that benefits society.

## Core values

- ethical and responsible AI
- open collaboration and knowledge sharing
- local relevance and community impact
- inclusion across research, education, and practice
- building capacity for the next generation of African AI leaders

## Community impact

AI-GAMNET is building a stronger AI ecosystem in The Gambia by connecting people, ideas, and opportunities. Through events, engagement, and collective learning, the community contributes to a future where AI is used for development, innovation, and social good.
