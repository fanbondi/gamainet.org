NASA Space Apps Challenge

Banjul Chapter

A global hackathon where people use NASA and partner-agency open data to develop solutions for life on Earth and in space.

About the challenge

Ideas, open data, and teamwork

The NASA Space Apps Challenge brings together people with different interests and skills to work on challenge statements created with NASA subject-matter experts. Teams use open data from NASA and its Space Agency Partners to explore problems in Earth science, space exploration, technology, and more.

You do not need to be a space scientist. Coding, research, design, storytelling, communication, and other skills can all contribute to a team project.

Current edition

NASA Space Apps Challenge

The hackathon is scheduled for November 14-15, 2026, with the theme The Next Frontier. Participants register through the official Space Apps website, choose a Local Event or the virtual Universal Event, and form or join a team of up to six people.

  • Explore the challenge statements and choose one that interests your team.
  • Work together during the hackathon using NASA and partner-agency open data.
  • Submit your project through the official platform by the published deadline.

Local event availability changes by edition. Check the official event directory for a location in The Gambia. If there is no suitable nearby event, the official site lists a virtual Universal Event option.
