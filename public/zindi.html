About Zindi

Learn and solve problems with data

Zindi is a professional network and challenge platform for data scientists and AI builders. Its challenges are developed with organisations such as businesses, NGOs, and governments, and invite participants to create data-driven solutions to practical problems.

Challenge topics can include areas such as agriculture, health, transport, and climate. Zindi also provides learning and community opportunities alongside its competitions.

How to take part

Choose a challenge and build your skills

  1. Browse the open challenges and choose a topic that interests you.
  2. Read the challenge description, eligibility requirements, rules, and deadlines.
  3. Use the challenge materials to develop a solution and submit it through Zindi before the deadline.
  4. Learn from the challenge community and review your result on the platform.

Requirements, team rules, evaluation, prizes, and deadlines vary by challenge. Check each challenge page for its current details.
